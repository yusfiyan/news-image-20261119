# The World in One Day — Thursday 19 November 2026

One 1024×1024 newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in six stories: the Gulf war widening as US jets strike a missile site on Qeshm Island and Iran claims hits on a base in Kuwait and radar in Bahrain; the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen; Hakeem Jeffries nominated to become the first Black Speaker of the US House; Grand Theft Auto VI launching worldwide after thirteen years; the COP31 draft text under fire in Antalya two days before the deadline; and Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's Andhra coast. An "Also today" strip rounds out the day with Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth, the EU's proposed €45bn loan for Ukraine, and Nvidia's acting CEO promising earlier pay raises. The image is rendered once, checked for size and dimensions, and copied to the output directory by `main.ts`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This validates `entry.png` (PNG signature, 1024×1024 pixels, at most 1 MiB, valid IEND trailer) and copies it to `out/entry.png`.
